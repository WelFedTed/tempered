A small QR code in the top-right corner. Scan it with a phone camera to open the group in the fit finder: every model the glass fits, screen sizes and fit notes. It's 11.6 mm square, the smallest size phones read reliably, so labels too short to fit it leave it off.
A barcode along the bottom carrying the label code with its glass letter, e.g. APP-1014-C. Clear, Privacy and Matte packs scan as different items, so your point-of-sale system can price them separately. Any USB or Bluetooth barcode scanner reads it. It needs a label about 39 mm wide or more.
Short warnings printed above the barcode, only on groups that need them:
FP-SAFE
Ultrasonic fingerprint reader. Use fingerprint-compatible glass.
COVER
Foldable. The glass is for the outer screen only.
2 SCREENS
Two-screen handheld. You need top and bottom pieces.
CASE-FRIENDLY
Sellers only list slightly smaller glass that leaves room for a case.
CURVED
Curved screen edges. Flat glass won't seal.
UV
Mostly UV-cured glass, fitted with liquid glue and a UV lamp.
MEASURE
Round watch. Measure the lens before fitting universal round glass.
Prints the price you type on every label in this PDF, in bold at the bottom left, above the barcode. Type it exactly as it should appear, e.g. $29.95. If Clear, Privacy and Matte sell at different prices, print each glass type separately with its own price.
